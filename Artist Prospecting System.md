# Artist Prospecting System

## Project Goal

Build an AI-powered prospecting system that identifies, qualifies, and enriches high-value female artist leads for international outbound outreach campaigns.

The objective is not simply to collect email addresses.

The objective is to build a continuously growing database of highly qualified female artists who are likely to participate in premium international exhibitions organized by our gallery.

Every collected lead should include enough publicly available information to enable highly personalized outreach, improve email engagement, increase reply rates, and maximize long-term conversion.

This system should produce a scalable prospecting pipeline that continuously generates new qualified artists for future exhibitions rather than supporting only the current campaign.

Success is measured by the quality of the collected artists, not by the quantity of scraped emails.

The resulting database should become a reusable business asset that supports future exhibitions across multiple countries over the coming years.
