## Ideal Artist Profile

The ideal prospect is a professional female visual artist who has both the financial capability and professional interest to participate in international paid exhibitions.

### Gender

Female only.

---

### Career Stage

Preferred

- Mid-career artists
- Established professional artists

Avoid

- Students
- Hobby artists
- Beginners
- Recently graduated artists
- International celebrities who are unlikely to participate in paid gallery exhibitions

---

### Professional Presence

The artist should have an established professional presence online.

Examples include

- Personal website
- Professional gallery profile
- Artist portfolio
- Instagram portfolio
- LinkedIn profile
- Artsy profile
- Saatchi Art profile
- MutualArt
- ArtFacts
- Exhibition catalog
- Artist CV

---

### Professional Activity

The artist should demonstrate an active professional career.

Examples include

- Solo exhibitions
- Group exhibitions
- International exhibitions
- Art fairs
- Museum exhibitions
- Biennials
- Artist residencies
- Gallery representation

---

### Financial Indicators

Preference should be given to artists who demonstrate indicators suggesting they can reasonably afford participation in international exhibitions.

Possible indicators include

- Multiple gallery representations
- International exhibitions
- Active collectors
- Professional portfolio
- Consistent exhibition history
- Strong online presence
- Representation by established galleries

---

### Language

English preferred.

Artists with strong English communication are prioritized because outreach will be conducted primarily in English.

---
